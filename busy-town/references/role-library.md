# Role Library / 角色库

The verbs and actors that physically perform a relationship. In a busy town, **the animal + its trade IS the verb**. Pick the role that can physically do the cooperation the grammar demands.

Two parts: (1) **animal species** (the body — pick fresh, not always cats), and (2) **trade families** (the verb — what the animal does). Combine one of each to make a townsfolk: a Fox Researcher, a Bear Miller, an Owl Inspector.

## Part 1 — Animal Species / 动物种属

Pick a species per role. Vary the cast — a town of only cats is a missed opportunity and risks drifting toward a single copyrighted character. Mix species across the page so roles are distinguishable at a glance.

| Species | read-as | leans toward trades |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cat / 猫 | quick, clever, hands-on | courier, baker, shopkeeper, inspector |
| Dog / 狗 | loyal, steady, public-facing | postman, police, fire, mayor, gatekeeper |
| Rabbit / 兔 | busy, many, fast | planner, runner, line worker, gardener |
| Fox / 狐 | sharp, research-y, sly | researcher, analyst, strategist, scout |
| Bear / 熊 | strong, patient, grounding | miller, smith, builder, warehouse, chef |
| Pig / 猪 | cheerful, hearty | baker, butcher, innkeeper, farmer |
| Mouse / 鼠 | small, precise, many | bookkeeper, watchmaker, sorter, clerk |
| Owl / 猫头鹰 | wise, oversight, night | editor, inspector, archivist, judge, mentor |
| Raccoon / 浣熊 | dexterous, hands-on | repair, mechanic, recycler, tinkerer |
| Badger / 獾 | stubborn, thorough | auditor, mason, foreman, archivist |
| Hedgehog / 刺猬 | careful, guarded | librarian, proofreader, archivist |
| Mole / 鼹鼠 | underground, hidden | miner, plumber, DBA, infra, "the layer you don't see" |
| Frog / 蛙 | jumping between | switchboard operator, ferryman, relay |
| Beaver / 海狸 | builder, structural | architect, dam-builder, infra engineer |
| Goose / 鹅 | noisy, flocking | herald, town crier, broadcaster, queue |
| Donkey / 驴 | patient, load-bearing | porter, cart-puller, long-running worker |

Rules:
- **No named/copyrighted character.** Never Huckle, Sally, Lowly Worm, Mr. Frumble, Bananas Gorilla, Sergeant Murphy, Miss Honey, or any likeness from the books.
- Give each species an **original trade outfit** (apron color, hat shape, scarf) — do not copy any recognizable character's specific costume.
- Pick species to **match the trade's nature** (a Mole for hidden infrastructure, an Owl for oversight, a Beaver for building). This makes the role read instantly without a label.
- A page should have **3–7 distinct species** for a busy cast; never a uniform species set.

## Part 2 — Trade Families / 职业库

Each trade is a verb the animal performs. Pick the trade that physically embodies the relationship (see `collaboration-grammar.md`). Each trade lists its **signature action** and **key prop**.

### Movement & Handoff trades / 传递类
- **Courier / 邮递员** — carries a letter/parcel from one shop to another. Prop: satchel, bicycle, parcel. Verb: hand off, relay, connect.
- **Porter / 搬运工** — lifts crates between a cart and a shop. Prop: handcart, crate, dolly. Verb: load, transfer, carry.
- **Ferryman / 摆渡人** — carries across a river/harbor. Prop: boat, oar. Verb: bridge, cross, translate between two sides.
- **Switchboard Operator / 接线员** — plugs cables between two stations. Prop: switchboard, cables. Verb: route, connect, mediate.

### Making & Transforming trades / 加工类
- **Baker / 面包师** — turns dough into bread (raw → cooked). Prop: rolling pin, oven, loaf. Verb: transform, produce.
- **Miller / 磨坊主** — turns wheat into flour. Prop: mill, sack, chute. Verb: refine, transform, funnel.
- **Smith / 铁匠** — turns metal into tools. Prop: forge, anvil, hammer, spark. Verb: forge, harden, transform.
- **Chef / 厨师** — combines ingredients into a dish. Prop: pot, ladle, stove. Verb: combine, synthesize.
- **Builder / 建筑工** — assembles parts into a structure. Prop: trowel, brick, scaffold. Verb: assemble, stack, build up.

### Sorting & Filtering trades / 筛选类
- **Inspector / 检查员** — checks items at a gate, stamps pass/fail. Prop: clipboard, stamp, magnifier. Verb: gate, filter, quality-check.
- **Sorter / 分拣员** — separates items into lanes by criteria. Prop: sorting screen, baskets. Verb: branch, classify, diverge.
- **Customs Officer / 关务员** — stamps approved, rejects others. Prop: stamp, ledger, gate. Verb: threshold, permit/deny.

### Measuring & Judging trades / 度量类
- **Scale Keeper / 司秤员** — weighs two sides of a tradeoff. Prop: balance scale, weights. Verb: weigh, tradeoff, balance.
- **Auditor / 审计员** — checks the books for errors. Prop: ledger, green eyeshade, quill. Verb: verify, audit.
- **Judge / 法官** — decides between two claimants. Prop: gavel, bench. Verb: arbitrate, decide.
- **Timekeeper / 计时员** — rings the bell at intervals. Prop: pocket watch, bell. Verb: cadence, schedule, trigger.

### Storing & Remembering trades / 存储类
- **Archivist / 档案员** — files and retrieves records. Prop: drawer, ledger, index card. Verb: store, retrieve, remember.
- **Librarian / 图书管理员** — shelves books by topic. Prop: shelf, ladder, card catalog. Verb: organize, index.
- **Warehouse Keeper / 仓库管理员** — stacks crates, tracks stock. Prop: crate, ledger, forklift. Verb: stock, buffer, persist.

### Routing & Overseeing trades / 调度类
- **Mayor / 镇长** — watches the whole town from a balcony. Prop: sash, balcony, telescope. Verb: oversee, govern, the whole-system view.
- **Crossroads Guard / 岔口守卫** — directs traffic at a fork. Prop: signpost, whistle. Verb: branch, route, diverge.
- **Harbor Master / 港务长** — directs ships to quays. Prop: megaphone, dock. Verb: converge, dock, route.
- **Town Crier / 报信员** — broadcasts a message to all. Prop: bell, scroll. Verb: broadcast, fan-out.

### Repairing & Tuning trades / 修复类
- **Repair Tailor / 缝补匠** — mends torn cloth. Prop: needle, thread, patch. Verb: mend, recover.
- **Mechanic / 修理工** — fixes a broken machine. Prop: wrench, grease. Verb: repair, recover.
- **Tuner / 调音师** — fine-tunes an instrument/machine. Prop: tuning fork, screwdriver. Verb: tune, calibrate, optimize.

### Guarding & Boundary trades / 守门类
- **Gatekeeper / 看门人** — controls who enters. Prop: gate, keys, lantern. Verb: boundary, permit, threshold.
- **Firefighter / 消防员** — responds to incidents. Prop: hose, helmet, truck. Verb: respond, contain, recover.
- **Night Watch / 守夜人** — patrols while others sleep. Prop: lantern, horn. Verb: monitor, alert.

### Hidden & Infrastructure trades / 隐形底层类
- **Mole Miner / 鼹鼠矿工** — works underground, unseen but essential. Prop: headlamp, tunnel. Verb: infrastructure, hidden dependency.
- **Plumber / 管道工** — keeps pipes (the data channels) flowing. Prop: pipe, wrench. Verb: plumbing, hidden transport.
- **Beaver Architect / 海狸建筑师** — designs the dam/structure everyone depends on. Prop: blueprint, logs. Verb: foundation, structure.

## Operator × Relationship Quick Map

When you know the relationship but not the trade, use this. (See `collaboration-grammar.md` for the full relationship list.)

| Relationship | Best trade families |
| --- | --- |
| Handoff & Relay | Courier, Porter, Ferryman, Switchboard Operator |
| Sequence & Assembly Line | Assembly Line Workers (any Making trade, lined up) |
| Dependency & Waiting | Waiter (any trade shown idle) + upstream Supplier |
| Causality & Trigger | Bell-Ringer / Timekeeper, Trigger Keeper |
| Feedback Loop | Loop Courier, Inspector, Valve Tuner |
| Contrast & Opposition | Two Mayors, Mirror Shopkeepers (same trade, two outcomes) |
| Tradeoff & Balance | Scale Keeper, Mediator |
| Hierarchy & Containment | Tower Keeper (cutaway), Floor Managers |
| Transformation & State Change | Miller, Baker, Smith, Repair Tailor |

| Divergence & Convergence | Crossroads Guard, Harbor Master, Funnel Miller |
| Simultaneous Busy | The Whole Town, Mayor on the Balcony |

## Trade Kit (the labeled tool set)

For a **Trade Close-up** mode image, lay out the trade's kit around the animal and label each tool. Each trade has a canonical kit:

- Baker: rolling pin, oven, flour sack, dough, finished loaf, apron
- Miller: mill wheel, wheat sheaf, grain chute, flour sack, sack stamp
- Smith: forge, anvil, hammer, tongs, finished horseshoe, spark
- Courier: satchel, bicycle, parcel, letter, route map, postmark stamp
- Inspector: clipboard, magnifier, approved stamp, rejected stamp, checklist
- Scale Keeper: balance scale, two weight crates, ledger, calibration weight
- Archivist: card drawers, index card, ledger, reading lamp, ladder
- Gatekeeper: gate, keys, lantern, visitor ledger, bell
- Mole Miner: headlamp, tunnel, cart, pickaxe, ore sack

The kit makes a single trade visually rich and self-explanatory without paragraphs of text.

## Casting Rules

- **One role per animal.** Don't have one animal do three things; the reader can't read it.
- **Reuse the same species+trade for the same role** across a series — the Fox Researcher is always the Fox Researcher. This makes a series feel like one town.
- **Vary species across the page** so roles are distinguishable.
- **Pick species to match the trade's nature** (Owl = oversight, Mole = hidden, Beaver = builder) — instant readability without a label.
- **Keep the cast small for a single image** (3–7 leads); a panorama can have many anonymous townsfolk but only a few labeled leads.
- **No copyrighted character.** If the user asks for a named character, recast as an original animal + original outfit in the same trade and say so.
