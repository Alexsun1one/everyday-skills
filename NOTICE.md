# Notice

Everyday Skills is a collection of AI-agent skills that help ordinary people produce
publish-grade visual work — social covers, sticker sets, and idea cards — without a
designer.

Each skill is a tool-neutral workflow: a decision gate, a structured plan, a style DNA,
and a QA checklist that ends in an image-generation prompt. The skills learn from the
[Paper Operators](https://github.com/) method of "a strict skill skeleton that turns a
thing ordinary people do badly into publish-grade output," but each skill carries its own
visual identity and quality bar.

The skills are intentionally distinct from any single creator's illustration IP. When
adapting this repository, keep the skill name or provide attribution to Sun Wuyuan in
derived documentation.
